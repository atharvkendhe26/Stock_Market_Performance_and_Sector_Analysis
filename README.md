**Stock Market Analysis Dashboard | Power BI**

**Project Overview**
  The **Stock Market Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze stock market performance using historical trading data. 
  The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of trading volume, stock prices, market trends, volatility, and sector-wise performance. 
  It enables investors, analysts, and business users to monitor market behavior, compare company performance, and make informed decisions through interactive visualizations.

**Purpose**

The purpose of this project is to transform raw stock market data into meaningful business insights using Power BI. 
The dashboard helps users analyze stock performance, identify market trends, compare industries, evaluate volatility, 
and support data-driven investment decisions.

**Business Problem**

Stock market datasets contain large volumes of daily trading information, making it difficult to identify meaningful trends and performance manually.
Investors and analysts require a centralized dashboard that provides clear insights into market activity, top-performing stocks, sector-wise comparisons,
monthly trends, and volatility. This project addresses these challenges by presenting complex financial data through interactive and easy-to-understand
visualizations.

**Tech Stack**

* **Power BI** – Dashboard Development & Data Visualization
* **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Data Transformation
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures & Calculated Columns
* **Excel / CSV** – Data Source

**Dashboard Features**

* Market Overview with KPI Cards
* Company-wise Closing Price Analysis
* Daily Stock Price Trend Analysis
* Price Change (%) by Company
* Trading Volume Analysis
* Monthly Closing Price Trend
* Bullish vs Bearish Market Distribution
* High vs Low Volume Analysis
* Volatility Analysis by Company & Industry
* Sector-wise Performance Comparison
* Interactive Slicers (Month, Company, Category)

**Key Business Insights**

The analysis shows that the stock market recorded a **Total Trading Volume of 74 Billion**,
indicating strong market participation with moderate price fluctuations. **TATAELXSI** emerged as the top-performing
stock by recording the **highest average closing price (~7.3K)** and also the **highest volatility (~32K)**, 
while **RITES** experienced the largest negative price movement (**-0.36%**).
Most companies traded at lower volumes, whereas only a few stocks accounted for exceptionally high trading activity.

The trend analysis indicates that the **Average Closing Price** gradually declined from **approximately 2.6K in September
to around 2.1K by March** beforestabilizing. Around **57.73%** of total trading activity came from **High Volume stocks**, 
while **55.93%** of stocks followed a **Bearish Trend**,reflecting slightly negative market sentiment.
Despite this, **60.68%** of the stocks were classified as **Low Volatility**, suggesting that the majority of companies 
maintained relatively stable price movements.

The comparative analysis highlights that **January** recorded the **highest monthly trading volume**, whereas **September** had the lowest.
The **IT Industry** showed the highest sector-wise volatility and market activity, while the **Financial Sector** had the largest representation 
among all sectors, followed by **Agriculture**. Overall, the dashboard successfully identifies market trends, top-performing companies, 
sector performance, and volatility patterns, enabling investors and analysts to make informed, data-driven investment decisions.
